A Quick Disclaimer

These four categories are not objective facts, and they are not built on any formal science or study. They are simply labels I have used for a few years to make sense of my conversations with a wide range of Latter-day Saints. They are imperfect, and they carry no endorsement from the Church or its leadership.

This quiz is my attempt at a useful and entertaining way to help people see where they might land. Like every quiz of its kind, it is imperfect. Some questions will not give voice to your exact perspective or capture nuance that matters to you. Choose the closest fit, and hold your results loosely.

There are no right answers. If a question is new to you, "not sure" is a perfectly good answer, and most questions include a Context note with background and sources. What each category means comes with your results. About 15 minutes.
